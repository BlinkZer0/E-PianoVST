E-Piano Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · applies to the E-Piano Android app, the E-Piano VST3 plugin and Standalone app for Windows, and the E-Piano web build

Summary

E-Piano does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no tracking. The Android app does not request internet access or any other permission. Everything you make in E-Piano stays on your device.

Who we are

E-Piano is developed and published by BlinkZer0 ("we", "us"). The source code is public at github.com/BlinkZer0/E-PianoVST, so you can check everything stated here.

What E-Piano does with your data

E-Piano never sends anything over the network. The only data it handles is created by you and stays where you put it:

DataWhere it is keptWhy
Presets and settings you saveAndroid app: the app's private storage. Windows: Documents\E-Piano\Presets. Web build: your browser's local storage, or a folder you link yourself.So your sounds are there the next time you open E-Piano
Audio samples you load (LOAD WAV)Read from a file you pick, then kept inside the preset or project you saveTo play the sample on an oscillator
MIDI messages from a connected keyboardUsed in real time, in memory onlyTo play notes, sustain, mod wheel and pitch bend
Interface state (for example which page is open)Local storage on your deviceTo reopen where you left off

None of this is recorded, uploaded, analysed or shared, and none of it contains personal information unless you put it in a preset name yourself.

Permissions

  • Android: the app declares no permissions. MIDI over USB and Bluetooth LE uses Android's built-in MIDI service, which needs no permission prompt. Files are opened only through the system file picker, one file at a time, when you tap LOAD WAV or LOAD.
  • Web build: your browser asks before E-Piano can use a MIDI device, and again before it can read a folder you link with FOLDER. Both can be refused and E-Piano still works.
  • Windows: the Standalone app and plugin access only the audio and MIDI devices you select and the preset folder above.

E-Piano does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or the internet.

Third parties

E-Piano contains no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics or crash reporting. A few services around the app are run by others and have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play handles installing and updating the Android app. Google may show us aggregated install statistics and, if you opted in on your device, crash reports. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Android backup may include the app's saved presets in your device backup if you have backup turned on in Android. That data goes to your own Google account under Google's terms, not to us.
  • GitHub Pages serves the web build and this page. GitHub receives the IP address of visitors in its server logs, as any website host does. See GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or look at these logs.
  • Your DAW or browser may keep its own data about the plugin or page (for example a plugin cache or browser history). That is governed by their policies.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored with us, deletion is entirely in your hands:

  • Android: uninstall the app, or use Android's Clear storage for E-Piano.
  • Windows: delete Documents\E-Piano and remove the app and plugin.
  • Web build: clear site data for this website in your browser, and delete any folder you linked.

Children

E-Piano collects no data from anyone, including children. It can be used by all ages, though please read the hearing note in our Terms of Use.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or export personal data an organisation holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will look into it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of E-Piano ever starts to collect data, this policy and the Google Play data-safety section will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/BlinkZer0/E-PianoVST/issues, or use the contact email shown on the Google Play listing.
