E-Piano Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · applies to the E-Piano Android app, the E-Piano VST3 plugin and Standalone app, and the E-Piano web build

Summary

E-Piano does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the Android app does not request internet access.

What stays on your device

  • Presets and settings you save are stored only on your device (in the app's storage, your browser's storage, or a folder you choose).
  • Audio samples you load with LOAD WAV are read from a file you pick and kept on your device, inside your presets or projects.
  • MIDI messages from a connected keyboard are used only to play sound in real time. They are not recorded or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • MIDI devices (USB and Bluetooth LE): used to receive notes and controller messages from a keyboard you connect.
  • File picker: E-Piano only opens files you choose yourself (samples and preset files).

The Android app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or the internet.

Children

E-Piano collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/BlinkZer0/E-PianoVST/issues.
